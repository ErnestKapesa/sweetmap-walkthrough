Why SweetMap

Great cakes are already being baked at home. They're just hard to find.

Many of the best cakes in town come from home kitchens. Usually you only find them through a friend, a WhatsApp status or a Facebook post. Ordering one means a long chat, sending photos back and forth, and wondering if it will be ready in time. SweetMap puts all of that in one place.

      Under the hood

      Built as a web app that installs to the phone home screen, so there is nothing to download from an app store.

      Location by area or GPS

      Type a neighbourhood like Kabulonga, or tap the crosshair for exact distance. Works fine when location is switched off.

      Light on data

      Photos are resized on the phone before upload, which saves bundles for bakers posting from the kitchen.

      Secure accounts

      Sign-in, photos and chat run on Supabase. SweetMap never stores passwords.

      Smart discovery

      Bakers who are taking orders, rated higher and followed more rise to the top of results.

      Kwacha pricing

      Prices, budgets and quotes are in K. Phone and WhatsApp numbers sit on every storefront.

      Admin controls

      An admin area can pause accounts and switch on maintenance mode, keeping the marketplace trustworthy.

      Questions

      Good to know

      Do I need to download an app?

      No. Open sweetmap.vercel.app in your phone's browser. You can add it to your home screen so it opens like an app, with nothing to install from an app store.

      How does payment work?

      You send an order request with your budget. The baker accepts first, and payment is agreed with the baker only after that, so nothing is due before they confirm.

      What if I need a cake tomorrow?

      Post an urgent request with what you need, when and where. Nearby bakers send quotes and you accept the one you like.

      Does it work in my area?

      Type any neighbourhood, or tap the crosshair to use GPS. Bakers list the areas they deliver to, and results are sorted by distance.

      Does it cost anything for bakers?

      Opening a storefront is free. Post designs, take orders and update statuses from the same account.

      Is my account safe?

      Sign-in, photos and chat are handled by Supabase. SweetMap never stores your password.
