      One order, two screens

      Every order moves through the same six stages. The baker moves it forward from their dashboard, and the customer sees each change on their tracking page straight away.

      Order received

      Customer sends the full brief: design, flavour, size, date, budget.

      Customer → Baker
      Accepted

      Baker confirms the job. Payment is agreed only after this point.

      Baking

      Customer

      Two account types, two toolsets

      People choose their role when they sign up. Each account only sees the tools that make sense for it, which keeps the app simple on both sides.

      Cake lover

      • Browse a feed of cake designs: For you, Following, Nearby
      • Filter by category, flavour or area
      • Find bakers near you, sorted by distance
      • Save designs and follow favourite bakers
      • Order straight from a design photo
      • Track each order stage and chat with the baker
      • Post an urgent request and pick from bakers' quotes

      Baker

      • A free storefront with gallery, followers and reviews
      • Dashboard of incoming, in-progress and delivered orders
      • One-tap status updates that customers can follow
      • Post designs with photo, price in Kwacha and tags
      • Set prices-from, delivery areas and a weekend special
      • Mark yourself fully booked when the oven is full
      • Quote on urgent requests from nearby customers

      Under the hood

      Built as a web app that installs to the phone home screen, so there is nothing to download from an app store.

      Location by area or GPS

      Type a neighbourhood like Kabulonga, or tap the crosshair for exact distance. Works fine when location is switched off.

      Light on data

      Photos are resized on the phone before upload, which saves bundles for bakers posting from the kitchen.

      Secure accounts

      Sign-in, photos and chat run on Supabase. SweetMap never stores passwords.

      Smart discovery

      Bakers who are taking orders, rated higher and followed more rise to the top of results.

      Kwacha pricing

      Prices, budgets and quotes are in K. Phone and WhatsApp numbers sit on every storefront.

      Admin controls

      An admin area can pause accounts and switch on maintenance mode, keeping the marketplace trustworthy.
